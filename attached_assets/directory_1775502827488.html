Transparency Statement · Equilibrium Vetting Protocol
The GlassHaus Resource & Practitioner Directory evaluates all external practitioners through an assets-first, risk-aware institutional rubric. Powered by Equilibrium. Composite score threshold: 5.5/10. GlassHaus has no referral or financial relationship with any external practitioner listed here.
  • Clinical credentialing — board certification, NAMS/ACOG/MSCP, specialty training
  • Cultural & ancestral competency — decolonized frameworks, anti-dismissal, diverse traditions
  • Patient advocacy — believes patients, does not gaslight, iterates toward resolution
  • Philosophical alignment — not pharmaceutical-first only, open to integrative options
  • Community rating — composite of BPN, Yelp, Google, community referrals
  • Ecosystem fit — would receive a referral from Priya without friction
Digital practitioners are AI-powered agents — identified by the (digital) suffix. Lumen Rewards pending Wyoming DAO LLC activation.
